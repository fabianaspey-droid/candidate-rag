Credit Controller | Ashby Pell Chartered Accountants (fictional) | Haverleigh

Ashby Pell is a fictional regional accountancy practice used to demonstrate this project. It has several offices and is growing by taking on smaller firms.

The Role
A client-facing credit control role in a professional services firm. You will work with partners and team leaders across audit, tax and advisory to resolve billing queries and keep cash coming in, without damaging client relationships.

What you'll be doing
Running credit control for the practice from invoice to receipt
Working with partners on billing queries and work in progress
Chasing overdue balances firmly and politely
Preparing aged debt reports and cash forecasts for the partners
Investigating disputed invoices and fixing billing errors
Bringing newly acquired offices onto the same credit control process

What we're looking for
Credit control experience, or someone keen to start a first role in finance
Confident speaking to partners and to clients
Enjoys getting to the bottom of a problem rather than chasing from a list
Organised, persistent and good at managing several people's priorities

Why join
A visible role in a growing firm
Flexible working
Competitive salary and benefits
