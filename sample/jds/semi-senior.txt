Semi-Senior Accountant | Ashby Pell Chartered Accountants (fictional) | Haverleigh or Marlow Cross

Ashby Pell is a fictional regional accountancy practice used to demonstrate this project. It is hiring a semi-senior to join a busy accounts and tax team.

What you'll be doing:
Preparing year-end accounts for a portfolio of small business clients, for senior review
Preparing VAT returns and personal tax returns
Bookkeeping and management accounts for a range of clients
Helping trainees and supporting seniors on client work
Dealing directly with clients to collect information and answer questions

Where this role can take you:
Over time you will take on your own clients and more responsibility for planning and running jobs.

What we're looking for:
AAT qualified, or studying towards ACA or ACCA
Experience of accounts preparation and bookkeeping in practice
Some VAT and personal tax experience
Comfortable with Xero, QuickBooks or Sage
Careful, organised and a clear communicator

Nice to have:
Management accounts experience
Experience of supporting trainees

What's on offer:
Study support towards ACA or ACCA
A clear progression path
25 days' holiday plus bank holidays
